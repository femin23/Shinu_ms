REFLECTIONS & ORIGINS

A Note from the Author

— SHINU M. S. —

What if there were a hell on Earth? What if that hell had a few sinners guarding it? What if they all had a different story to tell about justice? Jahanna was born from these wild thoughts.

The book, which I initially began writing under the name Parvathi five years ago, eventually transformed into Jahanna. Behind that transformation lies a long story of support and collaboration.

From the friends who listened to my ideas and encouraged me when I first put them on paper, to the publisher who helped refine those ideas and bring them to life, and the M Universe, which brought Jahanna into a shared universe alongside the Kanthamala Charitham trilogy and the Justice series — this journey has been filled with many wonderful people. My heartfelt love and gratitude to every one of them.

Jahanna is an experiment: an attempt to tell a story not about heroes, but solely about sinners. I have tried my best to do justice to the ideas, the words, the book, and its readers.

With the hope that Jahanna will satisfy you, I invite you to join me on this journey into the hell that exists on Earth.

With love, Shinu M. S.

SHARED LITERARY UNIVERSE

ERA UNIVERSE

One Universe. Countless Stories. A Shared Destiny.

ERA

Era Universe is a pioneering shared fictional universe in Malayalam literature, bringing together multiple writers to build one interconnected world of stories, characters, and mysteries.

Breaking away from the boundaries of traditional standalone novels, Era Universe introduces a collaborative storytelling experience where each author contributes a distinct narrative while remaining part of a larger, unified universe. Characters cross paths, events intertwine, hidden connections emerge, and mysteries unfold across different stories, gradually revealing a much bigger picture.

Fantasy Horror Mythology Dystopian Fiction

Blending fantasy, horror, mythology, and dystopian fiction, Era Universe gives writers the creative freedom to explore their own ideas while contributing to a shared overarching narrative. Every story adds a new dimension to the universe, creating endless possibilities for unexpected connections, deeper mysteries, and extraordinary storytelling.

More than a literary series, Era Universe is an evolving creative vision inspired by the possibilities of a multi-author cinematic universe. With ambitions to expand beyond books into films, web series, anime, and other forms of visual storytelling, it seeks to bring its interconnected world to life across multiple entertainment platforms.

“Era Universe is where individual stories become part of something greater.”

Era Universe is owned by Era Books Publications, which holds all rights to the universe and its intellectual property.

BIOGRAPHY

Shinu M. S.

Novelist & Creator of the Era Universe

Shinu M. S. is a visionary storyteller known for crafting psychological and supernatural tales woven into rich cultural mythos. With Jahanna: The Rise of Martha, he inaugurated the Era Universe, captivating readers with immersive suspense and philosophical depths.

His writing delves into the fragile line separating sacred devotion from primal fear, creating layered narratives designed to linger long after the final chapter is closed.
